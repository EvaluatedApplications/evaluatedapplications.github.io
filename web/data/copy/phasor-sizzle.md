# Sizzle copy: /phasor/ (the pattern for the other seven products)

The pattern, in order on every product page:

1. One vivid promise as the H1: short, confident, second person or imperative, no jargon.
2. A sub-line that says what you can build, in plain words, not what the product is made of.
3. Install line and a primary call to action ("See it run"), then the live demo straight underneath.
4. Number tiles: a big figure and a plain claim. Conditions and method sit behind a "See the numbers" disclosure.
5. "What you can build": benefits first, each with one proven number.
6. Run it yourself: the whole program, compiled and run against the published package.
7. Mechanism, coverage and proof (rungs, meter, credited bricks) as later, quieter sections.
8. Honest limits and "when another tool fits better" last.

Copy rules: short sentences, concrete verbs, active voice, no "harmonic", "syndrome" or "finite field" above the fold,
no hedging or apology, no history, every number real and sourced from an owner record or a recorded run.
