  • Doctrine

    Send your soldiers down the lanes and take their castle.

    A lane battle between two castles. Every soldier is its own body on a navigation mesh, and every 30 seconds the battle pauses so you can choose what to send and how to order it. Play the computer, or another person online.

    Play DoctrineHow it plays

    Each side's soldiers are items in one store, and the same choices in the same order always play out the same match, so a match can be checked by a short fingerprint at each pause.
