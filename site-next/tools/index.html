Tools

Run a real database and train a real model in this browser tab.

Our libraries, live. Nothing is uploaded: the work happens on your machine.

Start with Umbrarium

All tools

  • Umbrarium, preset demo

    The real Umbrarium dashboard computing exact answers over up to three million generated rows, entirely inside your browser tab. Click a chart to slice the rest, or run your own SQL.

    Powered byHoloDb

  • Doctrine

    Send soldiers down three lanes toward the enemy castle. The battle runs on a navigation mesh and pauses every 30 seconds so you can choose what to send next. Play the computer, or another person through a public relay, with no accounts.

    Powered byPhasorTracerEvalApp

  • The Helix

    Paste DNA sequences and a column of measurements and see the double helix in the numbers: a score graded in your browser, with the controls that would expose a false win shown beside it.

    Powered byHoloGenome

  • The Analyst

    Paste any data and profile it with a real SQL database in your browser: types, distributions, anomalies and hidden entities, then query it at a live SQL prompt.

    Powered byHoloDb

  • Prism

    A tiny language model that runs entirely in your browser: chat with it, have it write a story, watch one of its decisions on a map, then train your own copy on a foraging task or a price tape.

    Powered byAlgFormerPrismTracer

  • Prose

    Paste in a body of text, mine its grammar in your browser, and recombine it into sentences and question and answer pairs for a training corpus.

    Powered byProseHoloDbAlgFormer

  • Council Spending Scanner

    English councils' published spending data, prepared ahead of time and opened one month at a time, with the same mechanical checks for every council.

    Opens in your browser

The first tool you start downloads a runtime of about 11 MB. Your browser keeps it for the next one.
