Generated sentences pass the gate

Of 2,000 sentences requested in one run, 1,966 different ones came back. Every one passed the package's validity gate on a fresh parse, and every word in every one was observed in the corpus.

Where it is slower

Every loss in this record, stated once
  • The package returned 1,966 of the 2,000 requested sentences. It returns up to the number asked for and stops when it cannot find more different valid ones.

How it was measured

Stated once, for every figure on this page
Workload
8,232 sentences from 3 public-domain novels (Alice's Adventures in Wonderland; The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes; Pride and Prejudice) are mined, then 2,000 sentences are generated with a fixed seed.
Engines
Prose ProseEngine.Generate, checked with ValidityGate.Check and Attested.All
Check
Every sentence is parsed again by the same rules that mined the text and must have a real subject and a finite verb, with subject and verb agreement and article and noun agreement. A separate check confirms every open-class word was observed in the corpus.
Statistic
One run with one seed.
Ties
The corpus is large enough for generation to use observed pairings only.

Limits

  • The gate checks form, using the same rules that produced the sentences. It does not check meaning, and a valid sentence can still say something odd.
