Exact recall, refusal and repair

The exact key view recovered 64 items with no errors in 200 of 200 trials. Past capacity it refused every time and never answered wrongly. With 5 reserved keys any of 1 to 5 lost key harmonics was rebuilt, 300 of 300 times each, and one corrupted cell in either copy of a store was detected, 10,000 of 10,000 times.

What it costs

Every loss in this record, stated once
  • At 64 pairs the exact store is past its capacity and refuses every query, so it recalls none. It tells you it cannot answer instead of guessing.

How it was measured

Stated once, for every figure on this page
Workload
Checks of a keys-only store, the Pleroma's single-face special case. Exact recovery: store k distinct symbols from vocabularies of 4,096 and 2^20 symbols and read them back, at capacities from 1 to 96. Associative recall: store n key and value pairs from a codebook of 256 values and recall each value from its key. Repair and corruption: lost harmonics, one corrupted cell in either copy, and a lost store in a parity group, counted over the cases tried.
Engines
Pleroma keys-only store (CycleHologram); Pleroma with reserved keys, a check prime and parity groups
Check
Every exact decode is re-encoded and must reproduce every stored element before it is returned. Each repair and corruption check counts the cases that matched a brute-force answer.
Statistic
200 trials per capacity point. Associative recall uses 20 random seeds, with a chance level of 0.39 percent. Repair and corruption checks are exhaustive or random within the stated count.
Machine
CPU only, one core.
Memory and logging
Exact recovery is checked at about 2,064 and 2,580 bits of storage, and the recall table at 3,965 bits.
Ties
The exact read in the recall table is decode then test, not a ShiftKeys read. It measures recall, not model quality.
