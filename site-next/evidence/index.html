Evidence

What we have tested and measured, and how. Every record states its method once and lists what went against the library.

All measurements

  • Many small models, one wave: the evidence

    Six atoms that each see about a sixth of the text and share the medium score 2.99 bits per character. One atom that sees all of it scores 3.38, and the same six atoms without the medium score 4.33. At width 32, a pair scores 3.47 when the loss reaches the atoms through the notes they wrote and 3.84 when the writers are frozen. An eight-atom checkpoint scores 3.607 bits per token on 40 books outside its training lists, against 5.661 for a unigram baseline, and switching the medium off takes its atoms from about 3.7 bits to between 24 and 41.

    AlgFormerSmall-scale harness, six- and eight-atom checkpoints, held-out books and the fused GPU step

  • A gate holds its bound

    The gate never admitted more downloads than its bound: in all 7 recorded runs, with bounds from 1 to 24, the most downloads in flight equalled the bound.

    EvalAppA resource gate on simulated downloads

  • What HoloDb learns, and how it is tested

    After single-row updates and deletes the next sweep reads 6 blocks with learned records that adjust, against 60 and 51 when updates drop them, and 6 against 366 after consolidation. A wrong answer is the one failure a database cannot afford, so the tests try to make HoloDb wrong: across 8 customer runs and 158 sessions it compared 249 million rows against SQLite and recovered 46 times after a kill or a power cut, 37 times to the state before the interrupted write, 9 times to the state after, and never to a state between.

    HoloDbTest battery, chaos runs, customer lifecycle runs and engine counters

  • How HoloGenome scores against the lab

    A linear readout trained on 12,472 random-sequence constructs and tested on 82,368 yeast-gene constructs (no shared sequence, forward or reverse complement) scores r = 0.529 with vectors that use the measured twist, against 0.483 with one constant twist (difference 0.046, 95% interval 0.041 to 0.050). Adding the local-shape layer lifts it to 0.600, a further 0.071. A small phasor model that chose its own period, starting from 12 bases, settled at 10.49, 10.51, 10.48 and 10.51 bases per turn on four independent libraries, against the crystallographic 10.534.

    HoloGenomeHeld-out Loop-seq bendability measurements

  • Detail against components read

    One 16 by 16 chunk stored as a single hologram decodes 98.4 percent of its cells correctly at the full budget of 8,192 components and 131,072 bytes. Reading fewer components is smaller and fuzzier: 67.1 percent at 1,568 components, and 19.5 percent at 24, which takes 384 bytes.

    HoloVoxelOne chunk decoded at several budgets

  • Exact recall, refusal and repair

    The exact key view recovered 64 items with no errors in 200 of 200 trials. Past capacity it refused every time and never answered wrongly. With 5 reserved keys any of 1 to 5 lost key harmonics was rebuilt, 300 of 300 times each, and one corrupted cell in either copy of a store was detected, 10,000 of 10,000 times.

    PhasorExact recall, refusal and repair

  • Generated sentences pass the gate

    Of 2,000 sentences requested in one run, 2,000 different ones came back. Every one passed the package's validity gate on a fresh parse, and every word in every one was observed in the corpus.

    ProseGenerated sentences re-checked by the package gate
