Evidence

What we have measured and how. Every record states its method once, checks that every engine returns the same result before timing, and lists where we lose.

All measurements

  • What attention costs

    At the same shape and the same parameter count, reading a 1,984-token prompt takes 182 ms with softmax attention and 7.08 ms with holographic attention. Each further token takes 181 µs against 3.60 µs, and the serve cache holds 2,253 KB against 3.40 KB. Softmax reading grows with the square of the length (exponent 1.9) and holographic reading grows in step with it (1.0).

    AlgFormerTwo attention cores at one shape

  • What a visitor waits for

    On the sample app the static page downloads 6.8 KB before the visitor acts, against 2.8 MB when the same app boots the usual way. Real content is on screen in 72 to 100 ms on desktop and 416 to 484 ms on the phone profile, against 748 to 1,165 ms and 13.2 to 15.5 s. After the visitor presses Start the runtime takes 710 to 1,170 ms on desktop and 12.7 to 15.4 s on the phone profile to reach a working page, the same wait as the usual boot.

    BlazierThe sample app in headless Edge

  • What a gate bound does

    The gate never admitted more downloads than its bound: in every recorded run the most in flight equalled the bound. The time to finish 24 queued jobs fell from 1,113 ms at a bound of 1 to 139 ms at 8, then rose again to 410 ms when all 24 ran at once, because the simulated service slows when it is crowded.

    EvalAppA resource gate on simulated downloads

  • HoloDb benchmarks

    On a 10,000,000-invoice ledger joined to 200,000 customers, the dashboard rollups read in microseconds in the embedded engine, and every result is identical across the engines. The queries where HoloDb is slower are listed beside the wins.

    HoloDbEngine alone

  • How HoloGenome scores against the lab

    A linear readout trained on 12,472 random-sequence constructs and tested on 82,368 yeast-gene constructs (no shared sequence, forward or reverse complement) scores r = 0.529 with vectors that use the measured twist, against 0.483 with one constant twist (difference 0.046, 95% interval 0.041 to 0.050). Adding the local-shape layer lifts it to 0.600, a further 0.071. A small phasor model that chose its own period, starting from 12 bases, settled at 10.49, 10.51, 10.48 and 10.51 bases per turn on four independent libraries, against the crystallographic 10.534. The best trained network scores higher: the 22-parameter fit trails it by 0.28 to 0.39.

    HoloGenomeHeld-out Loop-seq bendability measurements

  • Detail against components read

    One 16 by 16 chunk stored as a single hologram decodes 98.4 percent of its cells correctly at the full budget of 8,192 components, 131,072 bytes and 17.0 ms. Reading fewer components is cheaper and fuzzier: 67.1 percent at 1,568 components, and 19.5 percent at 24, which takes 384 bytes and 0.09 ms.

    HoloVoxelOne chunk decoded at several budgets

  • Exact recall and refusal

    At equal storage the exact key view recovered 64 items with no errors where holographic reduced representations held 95 percent or better only up to 4. Past capacity the exact store refused every time, while the baseline answered wrongly every time.

    PhasorExact store against holographic reduced representations

  • Pleroma costs and checks

    A superposed kernel applies in 0.17 to 0.44 ms on a 10,464-cell store whether it holds 1 or 10,368 elements, and the repair and corruption checks recovered or detected every case tried.

    PhasorPleroma operations

  • Generated sentences pass the gate

    Of 2,000 sentences requested in one run, 1,966 different ones came back. Every one passed the package's validity gate on a fresh parse, and every word in every one was observed in the corpus.

    ProseGenerated sentences re-checked by the package gate
