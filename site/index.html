HoloDb

v1.7.7
EvaluatedApplications.HoloDb

An embeddable holographic database: data stored as one associative store and queried three ways over one copy — exact SQL by key, similarity by content (NEAREST), and constant-time aggregates. Real ACID with a write-ahead log, and a durable, larger-than-memory paged engine that spills to disk.

On a 10M-invoice ledger joined to 200k customers, the dashboard rollups resolve in microseconds and stay flat as the ledger grows — same SQL, same data, single-table and cross-table joins, verified identical against DuckDB and SQL Server. See the benchmarks →

dotnet add package EvaluatedApplications.HoloDb
Explore →Manual →NuGet

How it fits together

These aren't eleven unrelated packages. Phasor is the vector-symbolic core — the holographic math. EvalApp is the runtime that schedules and self-tunes the heavy work. HoloDb, AlgFormer, Tracer, and HoloVoxel are what you get when you build a database, a transformer, a game-AI SDK, and a spatial engine on that same foundation — and the rest extend those four: HoloDb.Client/HoloDb.Protocol put HoloDb on the network, AlgFormer.Gpu puts AlgFormer's holographic core on a GPU, EvalApp.Neural swaps EvalApp's tuner for a learned one, and Prose uses HoloDb and AlgFormer together to grow a training corpus.
