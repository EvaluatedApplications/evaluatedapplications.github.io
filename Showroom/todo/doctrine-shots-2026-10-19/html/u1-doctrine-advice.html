How it works

The army is a set of Pleroma stores from the Phasor package, the battle runs on a Tracer navigation mesh, the rounds run as EvalApp pipelines, and the general is a habit table with a tiny HoloFormer from the AlgFormer package.

Each cohort of soldiers is an item in a Pleroma store: its kind is the key, its traits are the flags, its place in the line is the arrangement, and its veterancy is a position on a ring of nine. An order is one operation on that store. Flip relabels a trait, Rotate relabels the line, Age ticks the ring, Aura counts the soldiers who carry a trait, and the list of orders you may give is read from the store, which is why some are greyed out. Field orders go to the battle: the ground is a baked Tracer navigation mesh, and each cohort is a group of agents that path, take cover and strike on it.

A battle is simulated once, on your device, and recorded. The replay is a recording of that one battle: playing it back does not run it again.

About the general

Your general starts empty. It learns how you set a doctrine from your own choices, in your browser, with nothing pre-trained, and different players end up with different generals. It suggests and you decide: every doctrine you set is an example for it, and so is each battle result.

Inside it are a small habit table and a tiny HoloFormer from the AlgFormer package. For each of the four slots the table speaks, unless the HoloFormer has been right on that slot at least two more times than the table over your recent choices, in which case it takes the slot. The match record shown is a score out of 1000 for how well its whole suggestion has matched what you then chose, over your recent choices. It starts low and climbs as the matches keep coming. Nothing is saved: refresh the page and the general is gone.
